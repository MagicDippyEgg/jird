Who is Jird?

Jird is jird. Jird is not a bird. Not a Russian spy. There is only one Jird. It speaks only Jird and is hopeful yet mysterious. Jird enjoys flesh, can revive people, and may pass on its soul. It is purple, enigmatic, and commands respect and fear alike.

Jird Land

Jird Land is a cruel yet whimsical realm of murder, sacrifice, and the supernatural. Residents and visitors report floating phenomena, strange whispers, and a constant presence of Jird’s influence. Sacrifices are said to please Jird and ensure its favor.

Abilities & Traits

  • Speaks only Jird
  • Revives people
  • Enjoys flesh
  • Can pass on its soul
  • Mysterious and hopeful
  • Purple in color

Cultural Impact

Jird has inspired countless myths, sightings, and local rituals. The phenomenon known as “Jirding” involves floating, strange encounters, and cryptic communication with the entity. People often travel to Jird Land seeking experiences or proof of its existence.
